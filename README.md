# RavenStack SaaS Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn, recurring revenue, subscription patterns, customer support performance, and high-risk customer segments for RavenStack, a fictional SaaS company.

Using SQL and Power BI, the project explores customer churn trends, revenue leakage, support metrics, and customer segments to identify actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze churned revenue across subscription plans and billing frequencies.
- Identify trends in customer churn and downgrades over time.
- Evaluate customer support resolution time and escalation rates.
- Analyze MRR and ARR across plan tiers and billing frequencies.
- Identify customer segments with shorter time-to-churn.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development, visualization and business analysis.
- **SQL** – Data querying and analytical investigation.
- **Excel / CSV** – Data handling and analysis.

## Business Questions

1. **Revenue Leakage & Subscription Patterns:** Which plan tiers (Basic, Pro, Enterprise) and billing frequencies (Monthly vs. Annual) are driving the highest MRR loss due to churn?
2. **Customer Churn Trends:** How has customer churn evolved over time, and during which periods did churn increase most significantly?
3. **Customer Support Performance:** How have average support ticket resolution times and escalation rates evolved over time?
4. **MRR & ARR Analysis:** How is recurring revenue (MRR/ARR) distributed across plan tiers and billing frequencies, and which segments contribute the highest churned revenue?
5. **High-Risk Segment Identification:** Based on acquisition source and industry type, which customer segments exhibit the fastest time-to-churn?


## Key Insights

- Enterprise recorded the highest churned MRR at approximately **428K**, closely followed by Pro at approximately **423K**.
- Monthly subscriptions contributed approximately **630K (53.41%)** of churned MRR, compared with approximately **549K (46.59%)** from annual subscriptions.
- Downgrade events increased from **1,107 in Q3 2024 to 2,069 in Q4 2024**, while churn events increased from **130 to 251** over the same period.
- Enterprise contributed approximately **80% of total revenue**, highlighting revenue concentration within this plan tier.
- Cybersecurity–Organic and other identified industry-source segments exhibited relatively short average time-to-churn, warranting further investigation and targeted monitoring.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention efforts for high-value Enterprise customers and investigate churn patterns among monthly subscribers.
- Investigate the increase in downgrades and churn events during Q4 2024.
- Monitor support resolution time and escalation rates to identify operational issues and improve support workflows.
- Protect Enterprise revenue through proactive renewal monitoring and customer engagement while evaluating revenue concentration.
- Introduce early-lifecycle monitoring and onboarding improvements for segments exhibiting shorter time-to-churn.
